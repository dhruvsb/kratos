RepVoice

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 August 2026 · Last updated 8 August 2026

RepVoice is a workout logging app. This policy explains exactly what it collects, why, who else can see it, and how to get rid of it. It covers the RepVoice iOS app and the backend it talks to.

The short version. RepVoice stores the workouts you log and the email address you sign in with. That is all. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising, no tracking, and no third party receives your data other than the hosting provider that runs the database. Nothing is ever sold. You can delete everything from inside the app at any time, and the deletion is immediate and permanent.

What data is collected, and how

Everything below is data you enter or import yourself. Nothing is gathered in the background.

  • Your email address. Collected when you sign in. RepVoice uses one-time email codes, so the address is both your account identifier and the only way to sign in. No password is collected or stored.
  • Your workout data. The routines you create, the exercises you pick, and the sets you log — weight, reps, RPE, set type, and the time each workout started and ended — plus any custom exercise names and workout notes you write.
  • Your preferences. A display name if you set one, and your preferred weight unit (kg or lb). Some settings, such as your weekly goal, are stored only on your device and are never transmitted.
  • Data you choose to import. If you use the Hevy CSV import, the workout history in the file you pick is read and stored as your own workout data. RepVoice reads only the file you explicitly select; it has no other access to your files.

RepVoice does not collect your name (unless you type one), location, contacts, photos, health or fitness data from Apple Health, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, or any usage, diagnostic, or behavioural analytics. It contains no analytics, attribution, advertising, or crash-reporting software of any kind.

RepVoice does not record audio. A future version is planned to support logging sets by voice; if microphone or speech-recognition permission is ever requested, it will be at the moment you use that feature, and this policy will be updated to describe it before the feature ships.

How the data is used

Your data is used for one purpose: to run the app for you — to save your workouts, show your history and progress, and keep you signed in. Your email address is additionally used to send you the one-time sign-in code.

Your data is never sold, rented, or shared for advertising; never used to build a profile of you; never used to train machine-learning models; and never used to send you marketing. There is no advertising in RepVoice.

Who else can access it

RepVoice is built and run by an individual developer. Only one third party processes your data:

  • Supabase — provides the hosted database and authentication that RepVoice runs on, and delivers the one-time sign-in codes to your email address. Your account and workout data are stored on Supabase infrastructure in Singapore (AWS ap-southeast-1). Supabase processes this data only to provide that hosting on RepVoice's instructions, and is contractually bound to protections at least equal to those described in this policy and required by the App Store Review Guidelines. See supabase.com/privacy.

There is no parent company, subsidiary, or affiliated entity with access to your data. Data may be disclosed if required by law, but you will be told unless the law forbids it.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. There is no separate retention period and no archival copy kept for analysis.

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account together with every workout, routine, set, and custom exercise on it. It happens immediately, it cannot be undone, and there is nothing left to recover afterwards. You do not need to contact anyone to do it.

Because signing in is what creates your account, deleting the account is also how you withdraw consent to any processing described here. If you would rather keep a copy of your history first, Settings → Data → Export workouts produces a CSV file of your workouts that you can save or share before you delete.

Backups taken by the hosting provider may briefly retain deleted rows before they age out on the provider's normal backup cycle; they are not accessible through the app and are not used for any purpose.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can access your data (it is all visible in the app), export it, correct it by editing it in the app, and delete it — using the in-app controls described above, with no request or approval needed. If you would prefer to exercise any of these rights by writing to a human instead, use the contact address below.

Security

All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted with TLS. Data is protected at the database level by row-level security policies, meaning the database itself enforces that a signed-in account can only ever read or write its own rows. No API keys or secrets are embedded in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but no data is stored that is not described above.

Children

RepVoice is not directed to children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13. No data is knowingly collected from children. If you believe a child has created an account, use the contact address below and it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date. Material changes to what is collected or who receives it will be announced in the app before they take effect.
